Scholarly Knowledge Graph for Citation Network Analysis

IIT Madras, 2025 to present

A knowledge graph over Information Systems academic literature, with a GraphRAG pipeline on top. It combines dense embedding retrieval with graph traversal to expand context, so LLM answers stay grounded in the papers and the citations between them.

  • Neo4j
  • GraphRAG
  • Embeddings
  • LLMs

Employee Sentiment Clustering Pipeline

IIT Madras, 2025 to present

Collected and analysed more than 1.6 million employee reviews from Glassdoor and AmbitionBox to surface new workplace sentiment categories and their drivers. The pipeline classifies reviews as HR or non-HR, then clusters them semantically. I compared MPNet against BGE and E5 embeddings, and built a custom annotation tool for blind validation.

  • NLP
  • MPNet
  • BGE / E5
  • HDBSCAN
  • UMAP
